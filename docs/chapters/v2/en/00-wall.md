# Chapter 0: A Wall of Problems

On this wall, each of the 7 principles crashes once, plus 3 combo problems. Finish a chapter, come back, tear one brick off the wall.

![Wall of video bloopers](images/ch00-wall.webp)

## 10 Problems on the Wall

**1. "The order is scrambled — nobody knows what I'm saying"** (Principle 1: time + sequence)
Every shot looks good, together they mean nothing. Chapter 2 fixes it.

**2. "The shot doesn't tell viewers where to look"** (Principle 2: attention)
Too much in frame, eyes have nowhere to land. Chapter 3 fixes it.

**3. "I filmed everything — 15 seconds of diary"** (Principle 3: omission)
No cutting, all footage. Chapter 4 fixes it.

**4. "The BGM buries the voice, and I never use silence"** (Principle 4: sound)
The sound layers are in the wrong order. Chapter 5 fixes it.

**5. "15 seconds that said nothing"** (Principle 5: change)
No change, just a screensaver. Chapter 6 fixes it.

**6. "Eyelines jump around — the viewer drops out"** (Principle 6: belief)
Direction, eyeline, action don't connect; one world collapses. Chapter 7 fixes it.

**7. "My prompt is vague, so the AI guesses"** (Principle 7: translation)
Feelings never got translated into shot language. Chapter 8 fixes it.

**8. "No storyboard — in the edit I find the story doesn't close"**
Shot first, thought later, order backwards. Chapter 9 fixes it.

**9. "The AI person looks different at second 1 and second 10"**
Consistency collapsed. Chapter 10 fixes it.

**10. "The first 3 seconds don't hold anyone"**
The hook didn't land; viewers scroll. Chapters 6 + 10 fix it.

## How to Use This Wall

Finish a chapter, come back, tear one brick off. When all 10 are gone, you can deliver a 15-second short on your own.

## Homework: Fill in the Blank (due tomorrow)

Pick the problem on the wall that hits closest to home, and fill in:

"The problem I most want to fix is #____, because I ____ (when it happened to me — e.g., my videos lose people in the first 3 seconds / my AI character keeps changing faces)."

Hand in: the completed sentence. In class tomorrow, we start with whichever problem gets the most votes.
