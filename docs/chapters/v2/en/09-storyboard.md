# Chapter 9: The Shot List — 7 Principles in One Table

**Map position**: Synthesis chapter · the working drawing for all 7 principles.

No storyboard, and in the edit you discover the story doesn't close. The director's working drawing is the "shot list": 7 principles, one table, everything in. Before shooting, fill the table in — once it's full, shooting never turns into "figuring it out on the spot."

![Shot list: one shot per row, one principle per column](images/ch08-storyboard.webp)

Look at this picture: the shot list has one shot per row and one principle per column — sequence, attention, omission, sound, change, belief, translation. Fill this table in and all 7 principles are "loaded into" your 15 seconds.

## What a Shot List Is

**One line**: the shot list = the working drawing for the 7 principles. One shot per row, one principle per column.

**For a 13-year-old**:

| Column | Asks what | You fill in |
|--------|-----------|-------------|
| Sequence | Which number? | Hook / buildup / twist |
| Attention | Where do eyes go? | Landing spot + shot size + movement |
| Omission | What gets cut? | Key frame (key frames only) |
| Sound | What sound? | Dialogue / effect / music / silence |
| Change | What change? | Hook / buildup / twist |
| Belief | Does the world hold? | Anchor words (character + scene + light) |
| Translation | What's the feeling? | Feeling word + prompt |

**Fill order**: fill "sequence" and "change" first (the story skeleton), then "attention" and "omission" (shot design), last "sound," "belief," and "translation" (execution details).

## Try It on a 15-Second Short

Your 15 seconds' shot list is your "permit to shoot": table not full, don't roll. Table full, and every prompt you generate is "backed by the table."

## Prompt Lesson: Shot-List Row → Prompt

One row of the shot list is one prompt. Stitch the 7 columns of a row together and you get the "complete prompt":

**Shot-list row**:
- Sequence: shot 1, hook
- Attention: landing spot = the cat's eyes, close-up, static
- Omission: key frame = eyes widening
- Sound: 1 second of silence, then a meow
- Change: hook
- Belief: anchor words = orange tabby cat, green eyes, white collar, milk tea shop, warm yellow light
- Translation: feeling = tense

**Complete prompt**:
- Chinese: 橘色虎斑猫，绿色眼睛，白色项圈，在奶茶店，暖黄灯光，猫的脸部特写，眼睛睁大，镜头静止，2 秒，感觉：紧张，顺序：第 1 个，钩子
- English: Orange tabby cat, green eyes, white collar, in a milk tea shop, warm yellow light, close-up of the cat's face, eyes widening, static camera, 2 seconds, feeling: tense, order: shot 1, hook

**Key**: one row of the shot list = one prompt — fill the table in and the prompts "grow out of it."

## Experiment You Can Repeat

For your 15 seconds, fill in one shot list (5 rows, 7 columns). Then shoot — and compare with the time you shot "without a table": does shooting feel like "you know what you're doing"? One filled table and the shot list clicks.

## Blooper Warnings

- **Empty-table blooper**: the shot list only has "picture" filled in, the rest blank — fill all 7 columns; only then is the table "complete."
- **Stale-table blooper**: you changed your mind while shooting and didn't update the table — changing your mind is fine; update the table first, then the shoot.
