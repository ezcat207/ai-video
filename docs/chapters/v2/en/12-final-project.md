# Chapter 12: Final Project — My 15-Second Short

**Map position**: Graduation chapter · 7 principles, one film.

The final project asks for: one 15-second short, all 7 principles passed, plus director's notes. Hand in the film + the notes, not an essay.

![Red-carpet premiere: a teen director's 15-second short playing on the cinema screen](images/ch11-premiere.webp)

## What the Final Project Asks

**One 15-second short**:
- Sequence: hook → buildup → twist, order right
- Attention: one landing spot per shot
- Omission: cut until nothing can be cut
- Sound: dialogue, effects, music, silence — layers right
- Change: one-sentence story, character from state A to state B
- Belief: eyeline, direction, action, light — four lines unbroken
- Translation: every prompt translated "feeling → shot language → prompt"

**One set of director's notes**:
- One-sentence story
- Shot list (5 rows, 7 columns)
- Principle self-check (7 principles, ticked one by one)
- Blooper log (how many times you crashed, how you fixed it)

## Principle Self-Check

| Principle | Check question | Tick |
|-----------|----------------|------|
| Sequence | Can I answer the three order questions? | ☐ |
| Attention | One landing spot per shot? | ☐ |
| Omission | Cut until nothing can be cut? | ☐ |
| Sound | Sound layers right? | ☐ |
| Change | Character from state A to state B? | ☐ |
| Belief | All four lines hold? | ☐ |
| Translation | Every prompt translated? | ☐ |

All 7 boxes ticked, and the short is watchable to the end.

## Director's Notes Template

**One-sentence story**: ____ (who) wanted to ____ (do what), and ended up ____ (the twist).

**Shot list**: (paste your shot list, 5 rows, 7 columns)

**Principle self-check**: (paste your self-check, 7 ticks)

**Blooper log**: the blooper I hit was ____, the fix was ____, the prevention is ____.

**Three-comment method**: send it to 3 friends and ask 3 questions — ① did the first 3 seconds hold you? ② did you know what it was about when it ended? ③ which shot do you remember best? 3 comments = "audience feedback."

## Homework: Fill in the Blank (graduation hand-in)

"My final project, in one sentence: '____.' The principle I found hardest was ____, because ____. The blooper I hit was ____, and the fix was ____."

**Example**: "My final project, in one sentence: 'A cat wanted to catch a mouse and ended up catching the remote.' The principle I found hardest was 'belief,' because 'the AI cat at second 1 and second 10 always looked like two different cats.' The blooper I hit was #8, and the fix was 'character sheet + reference image + short segments.'"

Hand in: the 15-second short + director's notes.

## What's Next

Graduation isn't the end — it's the start. You've loaded all 7 principles. Next you can shoot 30 seconds, 60 seconds, 3 minutes — the principles are the same, only more shots. The director's road only gets wider from here.
