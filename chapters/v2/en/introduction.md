# Introduction: Watch a 15-Second Short First

Same footage, two fates.

Left: a 15-second clip shot offhand — shaky camera, a sentence cut off mid-word, music drowning out the voice. You watch 3 seconds, you scroll.

Right: the director's cut — the camera pushes in slowly, the hook lands at second 3, the music pauses half a beat at the twist. You watch the whole thing, and you like it.

Same footage. The difference is the 7 principles in the director's head.

![Teen director holding a clapperboard on a homemade set](../images/intro-director.webp)

This book won't teach you to buy gear. Your phone + one ordinary AI video tool is the whole crew. What you're learning is the director's "translation": turning the story in your head into shot after shot, and then into sentences that can tell an AI what to do.

## The Rules of This Book

1. **7 principles, all the way through**: film = time + sequence; shot = attention; editing = omission; sound = half the movie; story = change; continuity = belief; director = translator. One principle per chapter.
2. **One running example**: a 15-second short (your TikTok/Reel) — every chapter tries the principle on it.
3. **Social currency hook**: every chapter answers "will this make my video hit harder?"
4. **Classroom flow**: concept → point it out on the picture → prompt lesson → experiment → fill-in-the-blank homework → what's next.
5. **Homework every chapter**: hand in one shot or one shot list, not an essay.
6. **AI prompt lessons**: 1–2 bilingual prompt examples per chapter (Chinese + English), ready to use.
7. **Tools stay generic**: no specific model is locked in — only the general way ordinary AI video tools work.

Watch the short first, then open the map. Chapter 1 loads all 7 principles at once.
