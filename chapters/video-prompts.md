# AI 视频书配图 Prompt（中英双语）

本书规矩：每张配图都有中文 prompt 和 English prompt，描述同一画面。中文 prompt 作中文版图，English prompt 作英文版图。

---

## 第一部分：本书插画 prompt

### 引言 intro-director.webp

- 中文：写实照片，一位十几岁的少年导演拿着场记板，身后是手机支架、补光灯和轨道，简陋但认真的自制片场，暖光，充满干劲。横构图，无文字，无水印。
- English: Photorealistic photo, a teenage director holding a clapperboard, phone mount, LED light panel and slider track behind him, humble but serious DIY film set, warm light, full of energy. Landscape composition, no text, no watermark.

### 第 0 章 ch00-wall.webp

- 中文：写实照片，一面贴满打印截图的"翻车墙"，截图上是各种AI视频翻车：一张脸在两帧之间变了样、凭空多出来的手、对不上口型的嘴、一团糊的运镜，一位少年抱着头站在墙前，暖黄灯光，幽默无奈。横构图，无文字，无水印。
- English: Photorealistic photo, a "blooper wall" covered with printed screenshots of AI video fails: a face changing between two frames, an extra hand appearing from nowhere, lips out of sync, a blurry camera move; a teenage boy holding his head in front of the wall, warm yellow light, humorous despair. Landscape, no text, no watermark.

### 第 1 章 ch01-map.webp

- 中文：写实照片，一面软木板墙上贴满彩色分镜卡片，每张卡片是不同的小画面：推镜、摇镜、剪辑时间线、音波形、故事曲线，中间一张大卡片写着数字15，一位少年的手正在贴下一张卡片，暖光，条理分明。横构图，卡片上无文字，无水印。
- English: Photorealistic photo, a corkboard wall covered with colorful storyboard cards, each a small scene: push-in, pan, editing timeline, audio waveform, story arc; a large card with the number 15 in the center, a teenager's hand pinning the next card, warm light, well organized. Landscape, no text on cards, no watermark.

### 第 2 章 ch02-movement.webp

- 中文：写实照片，一位少年推着手机稳定器向前走，镜头正在向前推进，前方是一位滑滑板的少女，背景有动态模糊的速度感，黄昏，电影感。横构图，无文字，无水印。
- English: Photorealistic photo, a teenage boy pushing a phone gimbal forward, camera pushing in, a girl skateboarding ahead, motion-blurred background with a sense of speed, dusk, cinematic. Landscape, no text, no watermark.

### 第 3 章 ch03-timeline.webp

- 中文：写实照片，手机剪辑 App 的时间线特写，几个视频片段排成一排，手指正要把一个片段剪短，屏幕泛着蓝光，背景虚化，夜晚的房间。横构图，无文字，无水印。
- English: Photorealistic photo, close-up of a phone editing app's timeline, several video clips in a row, a finger about to trim one clip, screen glowing blue, blurred background, night room. Landscape, no text, no watermark.

### 第 4 章 ch04-matchcut.webp

- 中文：写实照片，左右分屏对比：左边是一只手推开一扇旧木门，右边是一只手关上一扇现代玻璃门，两个动作形状呼应，像匹配剪辑的示意，暖光，电影感。横构图，无文字，无水印。
- English: Photorealistic photo, split-screen comparison: left, a hand pushing open an old wooden door; right, a hand closing a modern glass door; the two motions echoing each other like a match cut, warm light, cinematic. Landscape, no text, no watermark.

### 第 5 章 ch05-sound.webp

- 中文：写实照片，一位少年戴着耳机在笔记本电脑前剪辑视频，屏幕上是音频波形图，他正拖动一个音量滑块，桌面上有麦克风，夜晚暖黄台灯，专注。横构图，无文字，无水印。
- English: Photorealistic photo, a teenage boy wearing headphones editing video on a laptop, audio waveforms on screen, dragging a volume slider, microphone on the desk, warm desk lamp at night, focused. Landscape, no text, no watermark.

### 第 6 章 ch06-story.webp

- 中文：写实照片，一位少女站在白板前画故事线，曲线分成三段：开头一个问号、中间上扬、结尾一个爆炸星形，旁边贴着几张小分镜卡片，教室午后阳光。横构图，无文字，无水印。
- English: Photorealistic photo, a teenage girl drawing a story arc on a whiteboard: a question mark at the start, a rising curve in the middle, an explosion star at the end; small storyboard cards pinned beside, afternoon classroom sunlight. Landscape, no text, no watermark.

### 第 7 章 ch07-consistency.webp

- 中文：写实照片，九宫格分镜墙，九张小画面里是同一个短发少女穿同一件牛仔外套，出现在不同场景：街道、奶茶店、天台，脸和衣服完全一致，像角色设定表，暖光。横构图，无文字，无水印。
- English: Photorealistic photo, a 3x3 storyboard grid wall: the same short-haired teenage girl in the same denim jacket across nine panels in different scenes — street, bubble tea shop, rooftop — face and outfit perfectly consistent, like a character sheet, warm light. Landscape, no text, no watermark.

### 第 8 章 ch08-storyboard.webp

- 中文：写实照片，一张手绘分镜表手稿特写，六格小画面：猫特写、推门、追逐，每格下面有手写标注行（不写字，只有线条），旁边放着铅笔和橡皮，暖黄台灯。横构图，无文字，无水印。
- English: Photorealistic photo, close-up of a hand-drawn storyboard sheet, six panels: cat close-up, pushing a door, chase; blank annotation lines under each panel, pencil and eraser beside, warm desk lamp. Landscape, no text, no watermark.

### 第 9 章 ch09-editing.webp

- 中文：写实照片，笔记本电脑屏幕上是视频剪辑时间线，几个彩色片段首尾相接排成一条链，一只手正把最后一个片段拖进来拼上，像串珠子，夜晚房间暖光。横构图，无文字，无水印。
- English: Photorealistic photo, a laptop screen showing a video editing timeline, colorful clips chained end to end, a hand dragging the last clip into place like stringing beads, warm night room light. Landscape, no text, no watermark.

### 第 10 章 ch10-bloopers.webp

- 中文：写实照片，六格拼图，每格都是一张AI视频翻车截图：脸变形的少女、多出一只手的人、口型对不上的嘴、一团糊的运镜、风格突变的画面、音画不同步的波形，一位少年指着其中一格大笑，暖光幽默。横构图，无文字，无水印。
- English: Photorealistic photo, a six-panel grid of AI video blooper screenshots: a deformed face, a person with an extra hand, out-of-sync lips, a blurry camera move, a sudden style shift, an out-of-sync waveform; a teenage boy pointing at one panel laughing, warm humorous light. Landscape, no text, no watermark.

### 第 11 章 ch11-premiere.webp

- 中文：写实照片，一位少年举起手机向朋友们展示刚剪好的 15 秒短片，朋友们围着看，有人鼓掌，客厅像个小首映礼，彩灯暖光，欢乐。横构图，无文字，无水印。
- English: Photorealistic photo, a teenage boy holding up his phone showing friends his freshly edited 15-second short, friends gathered around, someone clapping, living room like a mini premiere, warm string lights, joyful. Landscape, no text, no watermark.

## 第二部分：本书 AI 视频教学 prompt（章节范例汇总）

各章"Prompt 教学"小节的范例，汇总在此，方便复制使用。

### 第 2 章：运镜

推：
- 中文：一位滑滑板的少女，黄昏的街道，浅景深，镜头缓慢向前推近，3 秒，速度均匀，不要晃动
- English: A teenage girl skateboarding on a street at dusk, shallow depth of field, camera slowly dollies in, 3 seconds, smooth and steady, no shaking

跟：
- 中文：少年在操场上奔跑，镜头跟随他横向移动，背景动态模糊，2 秒，速度感
- English: A teenage boy running on a track, camera trucks alongside him, motion-blurred background, 2 seconds, sense of speed

### 第 3 章：时长

钩子（短）：
- 中文：一只猫的脸部特写，眼睛突然睁大，镜头静止，2 秒，悬念感
- English: Extreme close-up of a cat's face, eyes suddenly widening, static camera, 2 seconds, suspenseful

情绪（长）：
- 中文：少年在天台上看日落，镜头缓慢拉远，5 秒，安静，留白
- English: A teenage boy watching sunset on a rooftop, camera slowly pulling back, 5 seconds, quiet, breathing room

### 第 4 章：转场（匹配剪辑留接口）

- 镜头 A 中文：一只手推开一扇木门，暖光，2 秒
- 镜头 A English: A hand pushing open a wooden door, warm light, 2 seconds
- 镜头 B 中文：一只手关上一扇玻璃门，冷色调，2 秒，动作方向与 A 呼应
- 镜头 B English: A hand closing a glass door, cool tones, 2 seconds, motion mirroring shot A

### 第 5 章：声音设计说明

- 中文：深夜的便利店，少年独自吃关东煮，镜头缓慢推近，3 秒，冷色调；声音设计：环境音压低，关东煮的蒸汽声突出，结尾处音乐骤停
- English: A late-night convenience store, a teenage boy eating oden alone, camera slowly pushing in, 3 seconds, cold tones; sound design: low ambient noise, prominent steam sizzle, music cuts out at the end

### 第 6 章：故事功能标注

- 镜头 1（钩子）中文：猫的脸部特写，眼睛睁大，2 秒；故事功能：抛悬念
- 镜头 1 English: Close-up of a cat's face, eyes widening, 2 seconds; story beat: hook
- 镜头 2（发展）中文：猫悄悄靠近一只老鼠玩具，镜头跟随，3 秒；故事功能：铺垫
- 镜头 2 English: Cat sneaking up on a toy mouse, camera following, 3 seconds; story beat: buildup
- 镜头 3（反转）中文：猫一爪拍飞电视遥控器，得意地看镜头，3 秒；故事功能：反转
- 镜头 3 English: Cat swatting the TV remote away, smugly looking at camera, 3 seconds; story beat: twist

### 第 7 章：角色卡

角色卡：
- 中文：短发少女，齐刘海，牛仔外套，白 T 恤，写实风格
- English: Short-haired teenage girl, blunt bangs, denim jacket, white T-shirt, photorealistic style

镜头 1：
- 中文：[角色卡]走在黄昏的街道上，镜头跟随，3 秒
- English: [character card] walking on a street at dusk, camera following, 3 seconds

镜头 2：
- 中文：[角色卡]坐在奶茶店里喝珍珠奶茶，窗边，暖光，镜头缓慢推近，3 秒
- English: [character card] sitting in a bubble tea shop drinking bubble tea, by the window, warm light, camera slowly pushing in, 3 seconds

### 第 8 章：分镜行 → prompt

- 分镜行：猫一爪拍飞遥控器 / 推近 / 3 秒 / 音乐骤停 / 反转
- 中文 prompt：橘色虎斑猫一爪拍飞电视遥控器，得意地看镜头，客厅暖光，镜头缓慢推近，3 秒，写实风格；故事功能：反转
- English prompt: An orange tabby cat swatting a TV remote away, looking smugly at the camera, warm living room light, camera slowly pushing in, 3 seconds, photorealistic; story beat: twist

### 第 9 章：补拍 prompt

- 中文：同一条街道，白天渐变为夜晚，路灯一盏盏亮起，固定机位，延时感，2 秒，写实风格
- English: The same street transitioning from day to night, streetlights turning on one by one, static camera, timelapse feel, 2 seconds, photorealistic
